# Core Loop

1. **Chop:** walk into the tree field; the axe orbits you and chops trees in range → logs on your back ([[Trees & Chopping]], [[Axe & Toolsmith]]).
2. **Deposit:** stand on DEPOSIT LOGS to feed the [[Tree Maker]] (3 logs = 1 tree).
3. **Collect:** COLLECT TREES picks finished trees off the tray (how many you can hold = CARRY MORE level).
4. **Sell:** SELL TREES sells to the front customer in the queue ([[Selling & Customers]]).
5. **Upgrade:** spend cash on pads ([[Shop Upgrades]]), expand the field ([[Field & Expansions]]), hire [[Workers]].

## Later layers
- [[Drive-Thru]]: cars buy several trees at once for more.
- [[Workshop & Decorating]]: decorated trees sell for 2.5×.
- [[Tree Lot]]: a display yard where shoppers buy on their own.
- [[Rebirth]]: reset for +50% on every sale per rebirth.
