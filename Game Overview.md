# Game Overview

**Genre:** tycoon / incremental, Christmas theme.
**Pitch:** you own a Christmas tree farm. An axe spins around you in the field and chops trees, you carry the logs to the Tree Maker, it makes Christmas trees, and you sell them to customers. The cash goes into expanding the field and shop, hiring workers and unlocking new ways to sell.

- Every player gets their own plot ([[Plots]]), saved on their account ([[Saving]]).
- Friends can join your plot and help ([[Play Together]]).
- Rebirth to start over with a permanent earnings boost ([[Rebirth]]).

## Style
- Blocky "studs" look, red/white shop, snowy ground.
- Lots of juice: chop debris, sell confetti, camera shake, bouncy pops (see [[Trees & Chopping]], [[Selling & Customers]]).
- Font: FredokaOne. Icons from the Vector Pro pack by RhosGFX (`TreeFarmConfig.Icons`).
