# Play Together

- Invite up to **3 friends** who are playing to your plot.
- Friend in the same server: an invite pops up; Accept moves them to your plot.
- Friend in another server: the invite goes through MessagingService; Accept teleports them into your server.
- Guests earn for the **host's** plot and save. When the host leaves, guests go back to their own plots.
