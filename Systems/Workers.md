# Workers

Hired on pads in the east shop wing; saved on the plot. **The wing was removed with the new layout (2026-11-17); Lumberjack and Tree Seller are now hired on the office PC (Staff app, [[Store Stock & Staff]]).** The Car Loader has no hire spot right now.

| Worker | Cost | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Lumberjack | 500 | chops trees in the field, brings logs to the [[Tree Maker]] |
| Seller | 2,500 | sells trees to customers |
| Car Loader | 6,000 | loads trees onto [[Drive-Thru]] cars (needs the drive-thru) |

- The Lumberjack enters the field through the gate on the middle path (`gapPoints`, measured from the plot pivot).
- Workers also raise [[Offline Earnings & Presents|offline earnings]].
