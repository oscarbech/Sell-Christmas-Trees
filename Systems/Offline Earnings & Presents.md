# Offline Earnings & Presents

## Offline earnings
Your farm keeps earning while you're away (min 2 minutes, up to 8 hours). Per minute away: tree price × (0.5 + 1 per Lumberjack + 1.5 per Seller + 1.5 per Loader). See [[Workers]].

## Presents
Gifts pop up around your plot; run into them for cash.

## TOP TREE TYCOONS board
All-time cash earned, global OrderedDataStore, refreshed every 90 s.
