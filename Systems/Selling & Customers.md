# Selling & Customers

- The **stand** is the counter at the front of the tree shop: one fixed price, the first and easiest way to sell. Widened on 2026-11-17: counter, sign and SELL TREES pad are 18 / 20 / 14 studs wide, the wall gap is x -9..9, the queue carpet is 6 wide.
- A queue of customers waits at the stand. They use the avatars of the plot players' Roblox friends, or random players if none are found.
- SELL TREES sells a carried tree to the front customer, who walks off with it; the queue steps forward.
- **Tree price** = 30 + 10 × shop level. Decorated trees × 2.5. Holiday Rush × 2. See [[Prices & Costs]].
- Juice: ka-ching, confetti, gold shockwave, "+$30" popup, cash icons flying into the HUD counter.

Other ways to sell: [[Drive-Thru]], [[Tree Lot]].
