# Rebirth

- Cost: **25,000 × (rebirths + 1)**.
- Gives **+50% on every sale** per rebirth.
- Resets cash, backpack and every level (field, shop, workers, carry, maker, drive-thru, workshop, shoes, toolsmith, lot).
- The drive-thru shop is taken down too (its BUILD buttons come back, see [[Drive-Thru]]).
- Keeps rebirths, totals, quests and your axe.
