# Drive-Thru

- Bought with the DRIVE-THRU pad (needs Shop level 1). Levels 2–3 bring more cars and better pay.
- Stud-built cars (`ServerStorage.Cars`) drive down the street and stop at LOAD CAR.
- Stand on the pad holding trees and they fly onto the roof rack. A full rack pays **tree price × CarPay** (2 / 2.5 / 3) and drives off.
- Car Parade event: cars come 3× faster ([[Events]]).
- The [[Workers|Car Loader]] can do it for you.
